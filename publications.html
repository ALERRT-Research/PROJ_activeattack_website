Publications

Research, reports and books that used the Active Attack Data, and a way to add yours.

The list starts with the ALERRT Center’s own work, from a 2013 book and the first FBI study in 2014 to the current articles. We would like it to grow: if you have published, presented or taught with the data, tell us below and we will add it.

Journal articles 7

  • Blair, J. P., Martaindale, M. H., Tanksley, P. T., & Johncox, J. D. (2025). Active attacks with motor vehicles: A short report and case study of the 2025 New Year's Day attack in New Orleans, LA. American Journal of Criminal Justice. doi:10.1007/s12103-025-09806-x
    Every vehicle attack in the data through 2024, beside the New Orleans case, the only 2025 attack in the data when the paper was written.
  • Blair, J. P., & Duron, A. (2022). How police officers are shot and killed during active shooter events: Implications for response and training. The Police Journal: Theory, Practice and Principles. doi:10.1177/0032258X221087827
    Officers shot during active shooter events, identified from the event data.
  • Sandel, W. L., & Martaindale, M. H. (2022). What are we talking about? Definitional confusion within active and mass shooting research. Journal of Mass Violence Research. doi:10.53076/JMVR47829
    Shows how the active attack definition differs from the mass shooting definitions in use, and what each one counts.
  • Duron, A. (2021). "Shots fired! We have many people down!": Common recommendations for managing high-casualty active shooter events in the USA. International Journal of Disaster Risk Reduction. doi:10.1016/j.ijdrr.2021.102548
    High-casualty events drawn from the data.
  • Blair, J. P., Sandel, W. L., & Martaindale, M. H. (2020). Correlates of the number shot and killed in active shooter events. Homicide Studies. doi:10.1177/1088767920976727
    Models of the number shot and killed per event: weapons, location, resolution, and year.
  • Martaindale, M. H., & Blair, J. P. (2019). The evolution of active shooter response training protocols since Columbine: Lessons from the Advanced Law Enforcement Rapid Response Training Center. Journal of Contemporary Criminal Justice. doi:10.1177/1043986219840237
    Event trends behind the changes in response training.
  • Martaindale, M. H., Sandel, W. L., & Blair, J. P. (2017). Active shooter events in the workplace: Findings and policy implications. Journal of Business Continuity & Emergency Planning. doi:10.69554/lcym6472
    Attacks at workplaces, 2000 to 2015.
