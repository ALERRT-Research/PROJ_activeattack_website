How to read the data

What counts as an active attack

An active attack occurs when an individual or individuals is actively killing or attempting to kill multiple unrelated people in a public space. The key word is actively: events that law enforcement learns of only after they end are excluded. Any weapon counts.

Attacker variables

Age, sex, and race carry a suffix for each attacker (age_1, age_2, age_3). For an attack with one attacker, the _2 and _3 variables are empty. The order is arbitrary.

Inclusive counts

Variables ending in _incl use the FBI’s more inclusive definition. ALERRT counts only people shot, wounded, or killed by the attacker; an injury suffered while fleeing is not in wound_tot.

Versions

Version 2025 was released September 8, 2026 and corrected November 6, 2026. Every change is listed under Updates on the Home page.
